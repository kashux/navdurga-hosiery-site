Our Story

Made for real bodies, real budgets, real comfort.

Navdurga Hosiery has been dressing women in soft, well-made basics for years — from everyday nightwear to pieces you feel good in under anything. We believe premium doesn't have to mean impractical: every fabric we choose is washed, worn and rechecked before it reaches you.

We've been trusted by our customers in Hyderabad for over 23 years, and that experience is what shapes every fabric and fit we choose today.
